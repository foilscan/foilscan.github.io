Support

FoilScan · 2026

FoilScan recognizes cards from 13 games with your camera, tells you their price and condition, and helps you organize your collection.

Have a question or something isn’t working? Email us at foilscan.app@gmail.com and we’ll get back to you as soon as possible.

FAQ

Where do prices come from? From TCGplayer, in US dollars, updated once a day. You can see them in your currency in Profile → Language and currency.

How do I cancel the subscription? In Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions → FoilScan Pro.

Where is my data? Only on your iPhone. FoilScan has no accounts or servers.
