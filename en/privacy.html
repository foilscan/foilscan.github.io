Privacy Policy

FoilScan · 2026

FoilScan has no accounts or servers of its own. Your collection, folders, wishlist, price history and scan history are stored only on your device.

The photos you scan are analyzed on the iPhone itself and are not sent anywhere. They are only saved to your photo library if you turn on “Autosave Photos”.

To show cards, photos and prices, the app queries public services: TCGplayer (via tcgcsv.com), TCGdex, Scryfall, YGOPRODeck, the games’ official websites (card photos in other languages), this website (Cardmarket prices and backup photos) and exchange rates from frankfurter.app. Those queries contain no personal data.

Price alerts are notifications from the iPhone itself and are checked on the device. The widget only shows your collection value stored on the iPhone.

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details. We use no advertising, analytics or tracking.

You can delete all your data in Profile → Delete my data, or by deleting the app.

Questions about your privacy? Email us at foilscan.app@gmail.com.
