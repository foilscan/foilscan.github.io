Privacy Policy

FoilScan · 2026

FoilScan has no accounts or servers of its own. Your collection, folders, wishlist, price history and scan history are stored only on your device. The scan history keeps your last 500 scans, and prices of cards you haven’t saved are kept for 60 days.

The photos you scan are analyzed on the iPhone itself and are not sent anywhere. They are only saved to your photo library if you turn on “Autosave Photos”.

To show cards, photos and prices, the app queries public services: TCGplayer (via tcgcsv.com), TCGdex, Scryfall, YGOPRODeck, the games’ official websites (card photos in other languages), this website (Cardmarket prices and backup photos) and exchange rates from Frankfurter. Those queries contain no personal data; as with any internet connection, those services receive your IP address. Downloaded data is kept on the device for a while to load faster and work offline; you can delete it in Profile → Clear Cache. Links to eBay, TCGplayer and Cardmarket open in your browser.

Price alerts are notifications from the iPhone itself: they are checked on the device, also while the app is closed (using iOS Background App Refresh, which you can turn off in Settings), and nothing is sent to any server. The widget only shows your collection value stored on the iPhone.

Purchases and subscriptions are handled by Apple. We never receive your payment details. We use no advertising, analytics or tracking.

You can delete all your data (including your last collection export) in Profile → Delete my data, or by deleting the app.

Questions about your privacy? Email us at foilscan.app@gmail.com.
