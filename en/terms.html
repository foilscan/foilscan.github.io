FoilScan is an unofficial app to identify and organize collectible cards. Pokémon, Magic: The Gathering, Yu-Gi-Oh!, One Piece, Disney Lorcana and the other games are trademarks of their owners. FoilScan is not affiliated with or endorsed by them.

Prices are for reference: they come from TCGplayer and are updated once a day. The estimated condition of a card is a guide and does not replace professional grading.

Use the app at your own risk.

FoilScan Pro subscription

FoilScan Pro is a yearly auto-renewing subscription. You can start with a 7-day free trial (if your Apple ID hasn’t used it yet) or a discounted first month; after that, the yearly price shown is charged.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID when you confirm the purchase or when the free trial ends. The subscription renews unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel it in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions.
